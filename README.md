## Antenna
[Ham Radio Study & Visualization](https://ham.charlesify.com/) - Interactive 3D visualization platform for Amateur Radio (Ham) antenna patterns and physics.

## Logs
[LoTW](https://lotw.arrl.org/lotwuser/default) - Operated by the American Radio Relay League (ARRL), the official online log database providing authoritative, automated QSL confirmation services for amateur radio operators worldwide.

## BBS
[QRZ](https://www.qrz.com/) - The most essential and authoritative online community and callsign lookup database in the global amateur radio (HAM) field.
